Frequently asked questions for employers

What is a salary advance?

A salary advance lets you receive part of your pay before your usual payday. If your employer offers Incash, check your account for the amount you can request.

How can I ensure that my employees use Incash responsibly?

Agree on how much employees can request and how often they can use the service. Incash can review those limits with your team during onboarding.

What amount would I be charged, as an employer, if I choose Incash?

Incash says employers can offer its earned wage access benefit at no cost. Employers can choose to cover some or all of an employee’s transaction fee. Ask Incash to confirm what applies to your arrangement.

Does Incash require any specific payroll system in order to provide its services?

Incash supports manual and automated payroll. Discuss your setup with the team so you know how deductions and reconciliation will work.

When do employees pay back an advance?

Your payroll team settles the advance on the agreed salary due date.

How can I get started?

Start in the employer portal. Incash will ask for company information and guide your team through verification and onboarding. Open the employer portal

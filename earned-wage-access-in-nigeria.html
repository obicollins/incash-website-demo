How can I get earned wage access in Nigeria?

Ask your HR team whether your employer offers Incash. Once your employer is set up, create and verify your account to check what you can access. Open Incash

Is earned wage access a loan?

Incash EWA gives you early access to pay you’ve already earned. The service is interest-free, and a transaction fee may apply.

Which companies offer earned wage access in Nigeria?

Incash works with employers in Nigeria. Your HR or payroll team can confirm whether your company is signed up. Refer your employer

Does earned wage access affect my monthly salary?

The portion you receive early is deducted when your employer runs payroll. That reduces what lands in your account on payday.

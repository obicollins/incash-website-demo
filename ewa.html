What is a salary advance?

A salary advance lets you receive part of your pay before your usual payday. If your employer offers Incash, check your account for the amount you can request.

How do I sign up for a salary advance in Nigeria?

Check with HR that your employer offers Incash. Then create an account and verify your details. Open Incash

What is the difference between earned wage access (EWA) and salary advance?

Earned wage access is tied to wages you have already earned. Salary advance is a broader term for receiving some of your salary early. Check your account for the limit and repayment terms that apply to your request.

What is the repayment method for salary advances?

Your employer deducts the advance from your pay and sends it to Incash through payroll.

Does a salary advance affect my take-home salary after the pay period?

Yes. Your payday payment is lower because you already received part of it.

Can I withdraw more than my salary when I apply for a salary advance?

You can only request up to the amount available in your Incash account. That amount depends on the pay you have earned and the limits that apply to your account.

What is the repayment period for a salary advance?

Incash describes payday as the usual settlement date. Check the repayment date shown for your request before confirming. Salary Top-Up has its own terms.

Are there any benefits to taking a salary advance?

Getting part of your pay early can help cover a bill between paydays. Check the cost and what will be left in your next pay before deciding how much to request.

Does Incash offer loans?

Incash’s earned wage access service uses the pay you’ve already earned. Our team can explain the terms that apply to Salary Top-Up and other financing services. Read about Salary Top-Up

What is the repayment method and period?

With an advance settled through payroll, your employer deducts it on the agreed payday. Review the date and the amount shown before you confirm.

How can I get started?

If your employer offers Incash, sign in using the app or web portal. You can also use USSD by dialling *347*338#. Open Incash

How can my company sign up for a salary advance?

Refer your employer so Incash can speak with your HR or payroll team about getting set up. Refer your employer

What are the best salary advance apps and platforms in Nigeria?

Start with the options your employer offers. Compare what you can access, any fees and what will be deducted on payday. Incash works with employers in Nigeria to offer early access to pay.

Are there any earned wage access platforms in Nigeria?

Yes. Incash offers earned wage access in Nigeria through participating employers. See how Incash EWA works

Salary Advance in Nigeria

When a bill is due before payday, a salary advance lets you request part of your pay early. If your employer offers Incash, check what you can access and request an advance in the app or online.

Open Incash Refer your employer

What happens on payday?

Your employer deducts the advance through payroll. Review the amount and timing in Incash so you can plan for what will be left on payday.

How to request an advance

Check with your HR team

If your employer already offers Incash, create an account and verify your details. If they haven’t joined, you can refer them.

Review what’s available

Sign in to see how much you can request. Review any fees and the repayment date before you confirm.

Follow your request

Open Request History in Incash to see whether the advance has been approved or settled.

How do I sign up for a salary advance in Nigeria?

Check with HR that your employer offers Incash. Then create an account and verify your details. Open Incash

Can I withdraw more than my salary when I apply for a salary advance?

You can only request up to the amount available in your Incash account. That amount depends on the pay you have earned and the limits that apply to your account.

What is the repayment method for salary advances?

Your employer deducts the advance from your pay and sends it to Incash through payroll.

Does a salary advance affect my take-home salary after the pay period?

Yes. Your payday payment is lower because you already received part of it.

How much can I get with Incash Salary Top-Up?

Eligible employees can access up to three times their monthly salary. For example, if you earn ₦200,000 a month, three times your salary is ₦600,000. The amount Incash approves may be lower.

What can I use Salary Top-Up for?

Incash offers Salary Top-Up to employees in Nigeria for larger expenses such as rent, car repairs or family costs.

How is Salary Top-Up different from earned wage access?

Earned wage access lets you request a portion of pay you have already earned. Salary Top-Up provides separate salary-linked financing with an agreed repayment schedule. See how earned wage access works

How do I enquire about Salary Top-Up?

Contact Incash to check eligibility, what information you need to provide, the full cost and how to apply. If your employer hasn’t joined Incash, you can refer them. Refer your employer
